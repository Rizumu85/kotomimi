## 更新内容

- 改进：个人词典按读音找名字，现在覆盖所有文字：英文名拼法不同（Risom → Rizum）、被识别成片假名、汉字、韩文或俄文（リズム、里祖姆、리점 → Rizum）也会改回词典里的写法。
- 改进：「回声消除」在 Kotomimi 服务下也会显示，默认改为「仅 Kotomimi」。以前默认消除所有声音，别人说话时会把你的声音一起压低，听起来像识别停了。用音箱时可以改回「全部声音」。
- 改进：用另一台 Kotomimi 翻译时，会带上上一句话作为上下文，会话开始时就先加载模型，第一句不再慢或失败。
- 修复：用另一台 Kotomimi 翻译时，繁体中文被翻成简体；开着「检测对方语言」时被当成固定语言翻译。
- 修复：小模型（Qwen3-ASR 0.6B）有时把个人词典的词表当成说的话写出来，或者一句话不断重复。
- 修复：各种识别方式之间不一致的地方：语法反馈看到的名字没经过词典修正、借用手机或 iPad 识别时不检测语言、检查设备一次没回应就退回普通识别、另一台 Kotomimi 断句不按你的设置。
- 修复：本机的语法反馈没有长度上限，模型一重复就会一直写到超时。

## What's new

- Improved: the personal dictionary finds names by sound in every script: an English name spelled another way (Risom → Rizum), or written by sound in katakana, Han, Hangul or Cyrillic (リズム, 里祖姆, 리점 → Rizum), is put back as the dictionary writes it.
- Improved: Echo Cancellation is shown with the Kotomimi service too, and defaults to "Kotomimi only". It used to cancel every sound, which turned your voice down while others spoke, so recognition seemed to stop. Choose "All sounds" for loudspeakers.
- Improved: translation on another Kotomimi gets the sentence before as context, and its models load as the session starts, so the first sentence is no longer slow or failing.
- Fixed: translation on another Kotomimi answered Traditional Chinese in Simplified, and translated a detected language as a fixed one.
- Fixed: the small model (Qwen3-ASR 0.6B) sometimes wrote the dictionary's word list as speech, or repeated a sentence over and over.
- Fixed: where recognition paths differed: feedback saw names before the dictionary put them right, a lent phone or iPad was not asked to detect the language, one unanswered device check fell back to plain recognition, and another Kotomimi cut sentences by its own rule rather than your settings.
- Fixed: feedback on this computer had no length limit, so a looping model wrote until it timed out.
