## 更新内容

- 改进：VR 头显的开关单独成了一张卡片，在“设置 → 常规 → VR 头显”里，不再和 VRChat 的视频设置挤在一起。任何 SteamVR 游戏里都能用。
- 改进：手腕上的小面板改叫“手腕菜单”，上面的按钮在各种语言下都排成一行。
- 修复：新手引导里有一句提示在中文界面下显示成了英文。

## What's new

- Improved: the VR headset's switch has a card of its own, under Settings → General → VR headset, apart from VRChat's video settings. It works in any SteamVR game.
- Improved: the small panel on your wrist is now called the wrist menu, and its buttons stay on one line in every language.
- Fixed: one tip in the tour showed in English in the Chinese interfaces.
