## 更新内容

**语言**
- 通过局域网用 Mac 的 Apple Speech 识别时，「对方说」点亮两种语言的情况下，Mac 会同时听这两种和你的母语。对方说你的母语会照原话显示，不再被写成另外两种语言之一。
- 电脑和 Mac 都要更新到这一版才生效；只更新一边时仍按两种语言听。
- iPad 仍是每次听两种语言：这是系统对每个应用的限制。

## What's new

**Languages**
- Hearing through a Mac's Apple Speech over the LAN, with two languages lit under "They speak", the Mac now hears those two and your own. What they say in your language is shown as said, no longer written as one of the other two.
- Both the computer and the Mac need this version; with only one updated, two languages are heard as before.
- An iPad still hears two languages at a time: the system's limit for an app.
