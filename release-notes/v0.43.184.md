## 更新内容

**VRChat 对话框跟着朗读走**
- 对话框会等朗读真正出声再显示，不会再出现字先到、声音几秒后才到的情况。朗读跳过或没读出来的句子会立刻显示，不再白等。
- 一句话太长要分页时，按语言跟着朗读翻页：读到哪种语言，就显示那种语言的那一页，每页都带上你的原话。原文在上还是译文在上，照你的设置。
- 不开朗读时，对话框和以前一样。

**字幕和录音**
- 在字幕条上更正一句话时，这句会留在原处。
- 会话录音会保留当时的提示信息。

## What's new

**The VRChat chatbox follows the voice**
- A line goes up when its reading is heard, no longer seconds before it. A sentence the voice skips or cannot make goes up at once.
- A line too long for one page turns its pages with the voice, language by language: the page of the language being read shows, with your own words on each page, above or below as you set.
- Without reading aloud, the chatbox works as before.

**Subtitles and recordings**
- A correction on the subtitle strip keeps its sentence in place.
- Session recordings keep the notices of the session.
