## 更新内容

- 改进：翻译语言重新设计。「我的语言」「对方的语言」下面各有一行：「我也会说」可以加上你会说的其他语言，「对方也可能说」可以加上对方可能会说的语言，或者选「任何语言」。点「+」添加、点 × 去掉；能加几种由正在用的识别模型决定，加不了的时候就不会出现「+」。原来的「自动检测」和「我也说xx语」会自动换成新的设置。
- 改进：会话中你说的话只会跟随到「对方也可能说」里的语言（选了「任何语言」时不限）。
- 修复：语音识别交给另一台设备上的 Apple Speech 时，「我也会说」会列出所有语言，模型名也显示成 apple-speech。

## What's new

- Improved: the languages are laid out anew. Under your language and theirs, "I also speak" takes the other languages you speak, and "They may also speak" the languages they may speak, or "Any language". "+" adds one and × takes it off; how many fit is up to the recognizer in use, and where none do, there is no "+". The old Detect and "I speak X too" become the new settings by themselves.
- Improved: in a session, what you say follows the other side only into the languages under "They may also speak" (any, with "Any language").
- Fixed: with Apple Speech on another device hearing you, "I also speak" listed every language, and the recognizer was named apple-speech.
