## 更新内容

**新功能**
- 帮助里新增「录下会话」：把每次会话的声音和文字存在这台电脑上，事后可以回头查问题，并在对话里给出错的句子做标记。保留 7 天，最多 2 GB。

**VRChat**
- 聊天框关着时说的话，重新打开后不会再被发出去。
- 一行就能放下的翻译，在聊天框里停留的时间和分页的一样长，来得及看完。

**识别和翻译**
- 一段话里混着两种语言时，每一句按它自己的语言来翻译，不再整段都当成同一种语言。
- 「20日」「14日」这类数字写成全角时，读音和罗马音不再出错。
- 开着语法反馈、自己也会说对方的语言、对方又点亮了第二种语言时，检查不再漏掉一个翻译方向。
- 个别服务的翻译或反馈结尾少一截的问题修好了。

**其他**
- 字幕条放在第二块屏幕上，下次打开还在那块屏幕。
- 搜索到的手机里，内存不够装的模型会标出来，自动选模型时也会避开。
- 后台偶尔出错时，应用不会再整个退出。
- 换模型存放位置时，只是看看还没确认的地方不会留下空文件夹。
- 菜单、关于窗口和系统的权限提示里，应用名都写成 Kotomimi。

## What's new

**New**
- "Record sessions" in Help: each session's sound and text is kept on this computer to look back at what went wrong, and a line can be marked as wrong from the conversation. Kept 7 days, 2 GB at most.

**VRChat**
- What you said while the chatbox was off is no longer sent once it is turned on again.
- A translation that fits one line stays in the chatbox as long as a page of the same length.

**Recognition and translation**
- A stretch in two languages is translated sentence by sentence, each by its own language.
- Numbers in full-width digits ("２０日", "１４日") are read and romanized right.
- With grammar feedback on, your own second language and a second language of theirs lit, the check no longer misses one translation direction.
- An answer from some services no longer loses its end.

**Also**
- A subtitle strip left on a second screen opens there again.
- A model a found phone has not the memory for is marked, and passed over when one is chosen for you.
- A failure in the background no longer closes the whole app.
- Looking at a place for the models without choosing it leaves no empty folder behind.
- The menu, the About window and the system's permission prompts name the app Kotomimi.
