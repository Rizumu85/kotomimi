## 更新内容

**识别**
- 在「我也会说」里选了语言时，你说的话如果被听成了别的语言（比如韩语被写成印地文或日文），会按你会说的语言重新识别一次；还是不对的那一句不再显示。
- 一句话说完后字幕定稿更快：除了看文字，也听声音来判断这句话是不是说完了。

**翻译**
- 新的本机翻译模型 Hunyuan MT 2 StreamRevise：对方还在说就开始出译文，一句说完再整句重翻一次。只支持中文、英语和日语。

## What's new

**Recognition**
- With languages chosen under "I also speak", a sentence of yours heard as some other language (Korean written as Hindi or Japanese, say) is recognized once more in the languages you speak; one that still comes out wrong is no longer shown.
- A finished sentence settles sooner: the voice, not only the words, tells whether you are done.

**Translation**
- A new on-device translation model, Hunyuan MT 2 StreamRevise: it starts translating while the other side is still speaking, then translates the whole sentence once more when it ends. Chinese, English and Japanese only.
