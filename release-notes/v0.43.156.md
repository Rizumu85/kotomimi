## 更新内容

- 修复："另一台设备"断开一阵子后要重新搜索才能选；平板断开后会变成"（手机）"，语音识别那里也选不了它。现在搜到过的设备都会记住，暂时连不上的也还能选，会标着"暂时连不上"，连上后自动恢复；设备一律显示它自己的名字（比如 iPad、MacBook），提示里也不再说"手机"。
- 头显字幕：抬头后再平视，字幕会回到原来的高度，不会停在高处；腕表"调整"页可以选字幕高度（高 / 中 / 低）。
- 腕表重新排版：按钮缩成两排，左边是对方的语言，右边是暂停、头显字幕、头顶显示、调整四个图标按钮，字幕区更大、窗口大小也不会跟着变；"调整"页也能直接选对方说的语言。
- 腕表的头显字幕按钮和软件里的"头显字幕"开关现在是同一个开关：在腕表上隐藏后，在软件里打开也能重新显示，不会再两边对不上。

## What's new

- Fixed: a device under "another device" had to be searched for again after it was away for a while; a tablet came back as "(phone)", and the speech recognition card could not choose it. Every device found is now kept: one not answering stays selectable, marked as such, and is used again once it answers. Devices go by their own names (an iPad, a MacBook), in the notices too.
- Headset captions: after a look up, they come back to their height when you look level again, instead of staying high. The wrist panel's Adjust page sets their height: high, middle or low.
- The wrist panel is laid out again: two rows of buttons, their languages on the left and four icon buttons on the right (pause, headset captions, chatbox, adjust), so the captions get more room and the window keeps its size. The Adjust page lists their languages too.
- The wrist panel's headset captions button and the app's "Headset captions" switch are now one switch: captions hidden from the wrist come back when the app turns them on.
