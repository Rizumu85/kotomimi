## 更新内容

- 设置整理成「常规、音频、提供商」三页，常用的放在前面；控件的样式、高度和选中状态统一了，浅色模式也一样。
- 字幕现在是一个独立窗口，不再把主窗口缩成一条，设置仍在主窗口里打开。
- 新增个性化词典（设置 › 常规）：记下容易听错的词，写上「听成」和「写成」，字幕和翻译都会用写对的词。
- 字幕：修好了不同语言下的多处显示问题，比如只剩罗马音、拼音被切掉、中文一直留在小字行；窗口不再在第一句译文出现时变高。两边选同一种语言时，字幕会显示得更大。
- VRChat：世界里放视频时可以选「只翻人声」「人声和视频都翻」或「先不翻译」，也可以每次询问。之前的「全都翻译」会盖掉人声，已经去掉，存过的设置会自动换成「人声和视频都翻」。
- VRChat：头顶文字按阅读时间发送，说得快时会把等待的句子合并，不再越积越多。
- 韩语罗马音按音节分开，比如 han-gu-geo，一段对应一个韩文字。
- 罗马音可以按语言单独关掉，比如只看韩语的；中文拼音也可以单独选字体。
- 语音：Mac 的 Apple Speech 共享给别的设备时支持「我也说××语」，一次听两种语言；不会标注语言的识别模型，会按文字判断语言，并在选项旁说明。
- 这台电脑上的模型现在都由应用自带的引擎运行，不再在本机启动 LocalAI；另一台设备上的 LocalAI 仍然可以用。如果之前用的模型不见了，到模型库重新选一个。

## What's new

- Settings are now three pages, General, Audio and Provider, with the most used first. Controls share one style, height and selected look, in the light theme too.
- Subtitles now open in a window of their own instead of shrinking the main window. Settings stay in the main window.
- New personal glossary (Settings › General): note the words recognition gets wrong, as heard and as written, and captions and translations use the right spelling.
- Subtitles: fixed several display problems across languages, such as only the romanization showing, pinyin cut off, or Chinese stuck on the small line. The window no longer grows at the first translation, and a run in one language shows larger captions.
- VRChat: when a world plays a video, choose People only, People + video or Pause, or be asked each time. The old Translate all drowned out people's voices and is gone: if you had it saved, it becomes People + video.
- VRChat: text above your avatar now goes at reading pace, and waiting lines are merged when you speak fast instead of piling up.
- Korean romanization is split by syllable, such as han-gu-geo, one part for each Hangul block.
- Romanization can be turned off language by language, and Chinese pinyin can have its own font.
- Speech: the Mac's Apple Speech, shared with another device, now supports "I speak another language too" and hears two languages at once. A recognizer that does not name a language now has it worked out from the text, and says so beside the choice.
- Models on this computer now all run on the app's own engine, and the app no longer starts LocalAI here. LocalAI on another device still works. If a model you used is gone, choose one again from the model library.
