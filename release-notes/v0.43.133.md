## 更新内容

- 语音识别交给另一台设备时，如果选的识别模型（比如 Mac 的 Apple Speech）没有你这次用的语言，选择框下面会直接写明缺哪种语言、去那台设备上下载就能用；这部分暂时由那台设备自己挑模型。

## What's new

- When recognition runs on another device and the recognizer chosen there (the Mac's Apple Speech, say) has no model for a language of this session, a note under the menu now names the language and says to download it on that device; meanwhile that device picks its own model for it.
