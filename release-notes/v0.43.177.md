## 更新内容

**朗读翻译**
- 「对方说」点亮两种语言时，自动朗读会把两种语言的译文都读出来，各用各的音色；朗读卡片里每种语言有一行音色。
- 你说的是对方的一种语言时，读的是译成对方另一种点亮语言的那句；译回你自己语言的那句只显示，不读。
- 打开「我的原声也送过去」后，只要 Kotomimi 开着，你的声音就一直送到虚拟麦克风，不开会话时 VRChat 或会议里的人也能听到你。
- 对话里每句的喇叭按钮只在鼠标指到那一行时出现。

**会话记录**
- 开着会话记录时，会多保存一份电脑播放的声音。

## What's new

**Spoken translations**
- With two languages lit under "They speak", the translations into both are read aloud, each in its own voice; the voice card has a voice row for each language.
- When you speak one of their languages, what is read is the translation into their other lit language; the one back into your own language is shown, not read.
- With "My own voice goes too" on, your voice reaches the virtual microphone whenever Kotomimi is open, so people in VRChat or a meeting hear you with no session running.
- The speaker button on a line shows only while the pointer is on that line.

**Session records**
- With session records on, what the computer plays is saved as well.
