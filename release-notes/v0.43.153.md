## 更新内容

- 头显里的字幕和电脑上的字幕一样了：两边各一条，原文小字、译文大字，开着语法反馈时也会显示。字幕像电影字幕一样停在视线偏下的位置，转头时平滑地跟过来，低头看手表或看地面时不会乱动。
- 腕表改成桌面上的一个小窗口「Kotomimi 腕表」：在 XSOverlay 里把它添加为窗口，就能戴在手腕上用，不会再和 XSOverlay 的腕表冲突。设置里的「VR 头显」卡片改成「字幕」「腕表」两个按钮，点一下打开，再点一下关闭。
- 头顶显示可以一键开关：腕表上有按钮，主窗口底部的模式按钮旁边也有一个（在 VRChat 卡片里选过一次头顶显示的样式后出现）。再打开时会回到上次用的样式。
- 修复：上个版本更新引擎后，有些电脑上的旧版引擎没有删掉，现在启动时会自动清理。

## What's new

- Captions in the headset now match the computer's subtitle: one lane for each side, the original small and the translation large, with grammar feedback when it is on. They sit low in your view like a film's subtitles, glide after you when you turn, and stay put when you look down at a watch or the floor.
- The wrist panel is now a small desktop window, "Kotomimi wrist panel": add it as a window in XSOverlay to wear it on your wrist, with no more clash with XSOverlay's own wrist. The "VR headset" card in the settings now has two buttons, Captions and Wrist panel, each pressed on or off.
- The chatbox above your avatar turns on and off in one click: from the wrist panel, or from a button beside the mode buttons at the bottom of the main window (it appears once you have chosen a chatbox look in the VRChat card). Turned back on, it shows the look you used last.
- Fixed: on some computers the previous engine was left behind after the last engine update; it is now cleared when Kotomimi starts.
