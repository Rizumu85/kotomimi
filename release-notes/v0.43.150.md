## 更新内容

- 改进：用 Qwen3-ASR 或 SenseVoice 识别时，会直接用模型自己听出的语言，跟着对方换语言更准，很短的一句汉字（分不清是中文还是日语）也能跟上。借安卓手机（需要手机端 0.9.5）或 iPad 识别时也一样。
- 改进：语音识别交给手机或 iPad 时，「我也会说」只会让你加设备上真有的语言；要用设备听不了的语言时，开始会话就会告诉你是哪台设备、哪个模型听不了，换语言时也一样，不会等到会话里每句都识别失败。
- 修复：模型偶尔反复写同一段、需要重新识别时，这一句听出的语言会丢掉。

## What's new

- Improved: with Qwen3-ASR or SenseVoice hearing, the language the model itself heard is used, so following the other side into another language is surer, even for a short line in Han characters alone (Chinese or Japanese). The same with an Android phone (phone app 0.9.5) or an iPad hearing.
- Improved: with a phone or an iPad hearing, "I also speak" offers only the languages the device has; a language it cannot hear is named at the start of a session, and at a switch, with the device and model, instead of every sentence failing once the session runs.
- Fixed: a sentence the model wrote over and over, and that was read again, lost the language it was heard in.
