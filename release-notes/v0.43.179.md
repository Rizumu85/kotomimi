## 更新内容

**用你自己的声音朗读**
- 朗读卡片选"这台电脑"，模型选 Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base（约 2.7 GB，占约 3 GB 显存），录十秒左右的一段话，或者导入一段已有的录音，译文就用你的声音读出来。不用训练，录完就能用。
- 每个音色第一次用要等几秒到二十几秒，之后一开口就出声。
- 一个音色可以补录其它语言：有录音的语言接着你的录音说，其它语言只取你的音色、不带口音。
- 音色可以改名，可以存成文件发给朋友，对方点"导入"就能用。拿到文件的人能用你的声音说任何话，只发给信得过的人。

**朗读**
- 朗读可以放在"另一台设备"：借用另一台开着共享的 Kotomimi 的朗读模型。
- 会话进行中可以换音色。
- 朗读卡片重新整理成三组：谁来读、用什么声音、给谁听。

**VRChat**
- 头顶对话框里的译文等朗读开始时才出现，文字和声音对得上。

## What's new

**Read aloud in your own voice**
- In the Voice card, choose This computer and the model Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base (about 2.7 GB, about 3 GB of video memory), then read about ten seconds aloud or import a recording you have: translations are read in your voice. Nothing is trained; it works as soon as it is recorded.
- The first sentence in a voice waits from a few seconds to half a minute; after that it starts speaking at once.
- A voice can have a recording in other languages too: a language with a recording is spoken on from it, any other takes only the sound of your voice, without your accent.
- A voice can be renamed, saved as a file for a friend, and taken in with Import. Whoever has the file can have anything said in your voice, so share it only with people you trust.

**Voice**
- The voice can be on Another device: it borrows the voice model of another Kotomimi that shares its models.
- The voice can be changed while a session runs.
- The Voice card is in three parts: what reads, in what voice, who hears.

**VRChat**
- A translation appears in the chatbox when its reading begins, so the words and the sound go together.
