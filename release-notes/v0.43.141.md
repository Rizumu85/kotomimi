## 更新内容

- 新增：VRChat 头顶显示多了「原文」：只显示你说的话，不等翻译，适合用来代替打字。
- 改进：在「语音识别」卡片里给一种语言换模型时，另一种语言如果也能用这个模型，会一起换过去；不能同时运行时，卡片上会直接提示，不再等到开始时才说。
- 改进：iPad 作为另一台设备时显示为平板，不再显示为手机。

## What's new

- New: the VRChat chatbox can show the original alone: what you said, without waiting for a translation, for using it in place of typing.
- Improved: choosing a model for one language in the Speech recognition card now moves the other language to it too where it hears that language; where the two cannot run together, the card says so at once instead of at Start.
- Improved: an iPad used as another device is listed as a tablet, not a phone.
