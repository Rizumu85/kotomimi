## 更新内容

**朗读更快、更稳**
- NVIDIA 显卡（驱动 580 或更新）会自动换用 CUDA 版引擎，识别和朗读都更快；和游戏一起用时朗读也更不容易卡。第一次会多下载约 850 MB，启动不了会自动退回原来的版本。
- 显存够用时朗读会自动开加速模式，显存紧张时不开。
- 自己声音的第一句等待时间大约缩短到原来的三分之一。
- 朗读跟不上时不再一顿一顿，改成整句生成完再播放，并提示一次。

**用自己的声音朗读**
- 同一个音色可以补录不同语气：开心、大笑、温柔、严肃、生气。朗读时会按句子里的词自动挑语气，没录的语气用平常的声音。
- 大笑只需要笑一下、说半句话，不用念长台词。
- 新增更轻的 Qwen3-TTS 0.6B Base，约 2 GB 显存，同样像本人。已经录好的音色两个模型都能直接用，不用重录。
- 借给另一台 Kotomimi 朗读时，较大的导入录音也能借出去了。

**识别**
- "识别跟不上"的提示只在持续变慢时出现，偶尔几句慢不再弹。

**其他**
- 把模型移到另一个磁盘在 Windows 上又能用了。

## What's new

**Faster, steadier reading aloud**
- An NVIDIA card (driver 580 or newer) now runs the CUDA build of the engine, so recognition and reading aloud are faster, and reading aloud stutters less beside a game. It downloads about 850 MB once, and goes back to the previous build if it cannot start.
- The voice's speed mode turns on by itself when there is video memory to spare, and stays off when there is not.
- The first sentence in your own voice waits about a third as long.
- A voice that cannot keep up no longer plays in bursts: each sentence plays once it is complete, with one notice.

**Reading aloud in your own voice**
- A voice can have recordings in other tones: lively, laughing, gentle, serious, angry. Each sentence's tone is picked from its words; a tone not recorded uses the plain voice.
- For laughing, just laugh and say a few words; no lines to read.
- New: the lighter Qwen3-TTS 0.6B Base, about 2 GB of video memory and just as like you. Voices you recorded work with both models as they are.
- Larger imported recordings can now be lent to another Kotomimi for reading aloud.

**Recognition**
- The "cannot keep up" notice appears only when recognition stays slow, not for a few slow lines.

**Other**
- Moving the models to another drive works again on Windows.
