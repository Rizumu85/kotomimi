## 更新内容

- 新增：两个翻译模型 PLaMo 3 Translate。2B 的译文读起来最自然；0.8B 最轻，只占约 0.6 GB 显存，适合一边开游戏一边用。默认仍是 Index-Translate，意思最稳。两个都仅限非商业使用。借安卓手机（需要手机端 0.9.6）或 iPad 翻译时也能选。
- 改进：语音识别和翻译用的引擎升级到新版。更新后第一次使用会自动重新下载一次引擎；Windows 上换成了兼容性更好的版本，较旧的电脑也能运行。

## What's new

- New: two translation models, PLaMo 3 Translate. The 2B reads the most natural; the 0.8B is the lightest, about 0.6 GB of video memory, for running beside a game. Index-Translate stays the default, the surest of meaning. Both are for non-commercial use only. They can also be chosen with an Android phone (phone app 0.9.6) or an iPad translating.
- Improved: the engines behind recognition and translation are updated. The first use after updating fetches the engine once more; on Windows the build that runs on older computers too is used.
