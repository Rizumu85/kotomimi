## 更新内容

**语言**
- 「对方说」里可以选你自己的母语了。只亮着它时，双方说的话都照原话显示成字幕，不翻译，适合听不清对方时当字幕用。
- 母语和另一种语言一起亮着时，对方说你的母语会照原话显示，说另一种语言照常翻译。
- 在「我说」里把母语换成对方正亮着的语言时，「对方说」会跟着调整，你说的话照常翻译。

**API**
- 选服务时密钥如果是从另一个环节带过来的，密钥框下面会写明它来自哪里。

## What's new

**Languages**
- Your own language can be chosen under "They speak". Lit alone, both sides are shown as said, as subtitles, with no translation: for when the other side is hard to hear.
- Lit beside another language, what they say in your language is shown as said, and the other language is translated as before.
- Making a language they have lit your own under "I speak" now adjusts "They speak", so what you say is still translated.

**API**
- A key taken from another stage when a service is chosen now says under the field where it came from.
