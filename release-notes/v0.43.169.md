## 更新内容

**语言**
- 你会说两种语言时，一句很短的英语不会再被当成中文了。以前这样的句子会被"从中文翻译"一遍，原样显示，也不给纠错。
- 用 Mac 或 iPad 的 Apple Speech 同时听两种语言时，纯英文的句子会用英语识别出来的文字，拼写更准。

## What's new

**Languages**
- When you speak two languages, a short English sentence is no longer taken for Chinese. It used to be "translated out of Chinese", shown unchanged, and got no feedback.
- With Apple Speech on a Mac or an iPad hearing two languages, a sentence in English alone now takes the English recognizer's text, which spells it better.
