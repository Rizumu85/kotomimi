## 更新内容

- 浅色主题：设置 › 帮助 › 外观，可选跟随系统、浅色或深色。对话窗口没改过的颜色跟着主题走。
- 新提供商 Qwen LiveTranslate（阿里云千问同声传译，用你自己的密钥）。
- Windows 上麦克风打不开时，会说清楚原因：被哪个程序占着、读不出来，还是系统隐私设置不允许，并给出对应的按钮。
- 本机语音识别：日语、韩语这类说完有明显句尾的话（「です」「ます」「요」等），停顿半秒就开始翻译，译文出得更快。
- 修复：一段话超过 8 秒后，偶尔会多出一行凭空的「はい。」。
- VRChat 里静音时，状态栏会提示「VRChat 静音中，不会识别你说的话」。
- 日语读音：日期里的「1日」读「ついたち」，「4月」「7月」「9月」读「しがつ」「しちがつ」「くがつ」；正在说的那句话，最后一个词的读音等说完再显示，不再跳来跳去。
- 字幕模式：已经自动处理的提示不再挤掉上一句的译文；开着罗马音时，下面一行不再露出上一行的半截。
- 修复：浅色主题下给对话窗口选的深色背景，重启后会丢失。

## What's new

- Light theme: Settings › Help › Appearance, following the system, light or dark. Conversation colours you never changed follow the theme.
- New provider: Qwen LiveTranslate (Alibaba Cloud's simultaneous interpretation, with your own key).
- On Windows, a microphone that will not open now says why — held by a named app, unreadable, or blocked by privacy settings — with a button for each.
- On-device recognition: a Japanese or Korean sentence that ends on a clear closing word (です, ます, 요…) is translated after a half-second pause, so its translation comes sooner.
- Fixed: a stretch of speech over 8 seconds now and then left a made-up line「はい。」.
- Muted in VRChat, the status line says that what you say is not being recognized.
- Japanese readings: 1日 in a date reads ついたち, and 4月, 7月, 9月 read しがつ, しちがつ, くがつ; the last word of a sentence still being spoken gets its reading once the sentence is over, instead of flickering.
- Subtitle mode: a notice about something already taken care of no longer pushes out the last translation; with romanization on, the lower line no longer shows half of the line before it.
- Fixed: a dark conversation background picked in the light theme was lost on restart.
