## 更新内容

**翻译**
- 修复：翻译模型偶尔会把很短的一句话翻成同一个词重复上千字，后面的句子要等十来秒，VRChat 头顶显示也被占住很久。现在一发现这样的重复就停下，只留下和原句差不多长的一段。

## What's new

**Translation**
- Fixed: now and then a translation model answered a short sentence with one word repeated a thousand letters long. Later sentences waited some ten seconds for it, and the VRChat chatbox was held up paging through it. Such a repetition is now stopped as soon as it shows, and about as much of it as the sentence is long is kept.
