## 更新内容

- 新功能：会话中可以直接换语言，不用先停。设置里的语言、VR 腕表上的语言和模型都能在会话进行中切换，之前的对话和麦克风都不会断。
- 新功能：对方的语言选「自动检测」时，你说的话会跟着翻成对方正在说的语言。同时和韩国人、日本人聊，各自都能收到自己的语言；连着两句或一句长话才会切换，不会被一两个字带跑。
- 新功能：翻译语言下面直接列出识别模型能听出的语言。「我也会说」可以选多种你会说的语言，「对方可能说」里能看到正在跟随的语言；旁边写着是哪个模型在听，点它就能去换。
- 改进：Qwen3-ASR 0.6B 和 SenseVoice 也能自动识别对方的语言了（SenseVoice 限中、粤、英、日、韩五种）。模型库里会把能自动识别的和要指定语言的分开显示。
- 修复：开着会话一直不关时，Kotomimi 会越来越占内存和 CPU（一晚能涨到 8 GB 以上）。

## What's new

- New: languages change mid-session without stopping it. The settings' languages, and the languages and models on the VR wrist, switch while the session runs; the conversation and the microphone go on.
- New: with the other side's language on Detect, what you say is translated into the language they are speaking. Talking with a Korean and a Japanese speaker at once, each gets theirs; it moves after two sentences in a row or one long one, not on a word or two.
- New: under the languages, the ones the recognizer can tell apart are listed. "I also speak" takes several languages you speak; "They may speak" shows the one being followed; the recognizer is named beside them, and pressing it opens its settings.
- Improved: Qwen3-ASR 0.6B and SenseVoice detect the other side's language too (SenseVoice among Chinese, Cantonese, English, Japanese and Korean). The model library lists the models that detect apart from those that must be told a language.
- Fixed: left running, a session made Kotomimi take more and more memory and CPU (over 8 GB in a night).
