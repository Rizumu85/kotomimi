## 更新内容

- 两边语言选成同一种（比如简体中文对简体中文）时，只识别、不翻译，也不加载翻译模型：适合只想把别人说的话听得更清楚的时候。简体对繁体这类不同写法仍会翻译。

## What's new

- With the same language on both sides (for example Simplified Chinese to Simplified Chinese), speech is only transcribed, not translated, and no translation model is loaded: for when you only want to follow what someone says more clearly. Two scripts of one language, such as Simplified to Traditional Chinese, are still translated.
