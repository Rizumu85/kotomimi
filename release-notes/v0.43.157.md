## 更新内容

- 对方点亮两种语言时，Apple Speech 会按这两种语言来听：以前它听的是对方的主语言和你的语言，对方说第二种语言时容易听错。Mac 会同时听三种（对方的两种加你的语言），对方说你的语言也能听出来；iPad 听对方的两种。
- "我也会说"里有正在用的另一种语言时，Apple Speech 听你的话也按这种语言配对。
- 用 iPad 识别时，如果 iPad 没下载对方点亮的第二种语言，开始前会直接提示，不会开始了却听不到。

## What's new

- With two of the other side's languages lit, Apple Speech now listens for those two: it used to listen for their main language and yours, and mishear the second. A Mac listens for three at once (their two and yours), so they can still speak yours; an iPad listens for their two.
- Where another language you speak is in use, Apple Speech pairs your speech with it.
- With an iPad hearing, a run whose second lit language the iPad has not downloaded is stopped before it starts, with a notice, instead of never hearing it.
