## 更新内容

- **我也说对方的语言**：翻译语言下面新增开关「我也说××语」。打开后，你说自己的语言照常翻成对方的语言，说对方的语言则翻成你的语言；在 VRChat 里，头顶的聊天框两边的人都看得懂。需要能自动分辨语言的识别模型（如 Qwen3-ASR 1.7B），和"我说对方的语言时检查语法"二选一。
- 识别交给另一台设备时，"语音识别"卡片会显示那台设备实际正在用的模型（与所选的不同时显示）。
- Mac 自带的语音识别最多同时保留 5 种语言；现在名额满时会自动让出不需要的语言，不再出现下载失败或会话无法开始。

## What's new

- **I speak their language too**: a new switch under the language pair. Your own language is translated into theirs as ever, and when you speak theirs it is translated into yours, so in VRChat both sides can read your chatbox. It needs a recognizer that detects the language (such as Qwen3-ASR 1.7B), and it cannot be on together with grammar feedback.
- When another device does the recognition, the recognition card shows which model it is actually using, where that differs from the one chosen.
- The Mac's own speech recognition keeps at most five languages reserved; when they are all taken, one that is not needed is now let go by itself, instead of a failed download or a session that would not start.
