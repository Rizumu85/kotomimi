## 更新内容

- 改进：头上显示的翻译会马上跟着对方最后说的语言走。对方说一句韩语，你接下来的话就翻成韩语；不用等对方连着说两句，也不限于「对方也可能说」里列出的语言。「はい」「ok」这种很短的回应和你自己的语言不会触发切换。
- 修复：声音很小或含糊时，字典里的词会冒到对话里。现在只要识别结果全是字典词，就会不带字典再听一遍；什么都没听到或只听到一点点，就不再显示这个词。真的叫名字时照常保留字典里的写法。

## What's new

- Improved: the translation shown overhead follows the language the other side spoke last, at once. One sentence of Korean, and what you say next goes into Korean; it no longer waits for two in a row, nor keeps to the languages under "They may also speak". A short "はい" or "ok", and your own language, do not move it.
- Fixed: with a quiet or unclear sound, words of your dictionary came up in the conversation. A reading of dictionary words alone is now read again without them; where that hears nothing, or far less, the word is not shown. A name really called is kept as the dictionary writes it.
