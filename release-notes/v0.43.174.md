## 更新内容

**语音识别**
- 「我说」和「对方说」可以各选一处识别：在「语音识别」卡片右上角点「「我说」另选一处听」。比如你这边交给另一台设备，对方那边用这台电脑的模型。
- 新模型 SenseVoice-Small CPU：只用处理器，不占显存，支持中文、粤语、英语、日语和韩语，和显卡上的 SenseVoice 差不多准。
- 给自己选的识别模型听不了「我也会说」里的某种语言时，不再拦着不让选：那种语言暂时不听，语言按钮上会写明原因。
- 修复：部分 Windows（如 Windows 10）上下载 X-ASR 时提示「The engine could not be unpacked」。

**语言**
- 你说的每一句都会显示这一场用到的全部语言，顺序固定：你的语言、对方的主语言、对方点亮的另一种语言，不管这句是用哪种语言说的。对话和 VRChat 头顶显示都是这样，打字输入也一样。
- 头顶显示放不下时分页发送，不再去掉其中一行。

**VRChat**
- 修复：VRChat 没开时也提示「在 VRChat 快捷菜单中打开 OSC」。

## What's new

**Speech recognition**
- "I speak" and "They speak" can each be heard in a place of their own: press "Hear my side somewhere else" at the top right of the speech recognition card. For example, your side on another device and theirs on this computer's model.
- New model, SenseVoice-Small CPU: on the processor alone, with no video memory taken. Chinese, Cantonese, English, Japanese and Korean, about as accurate as SenseVoice on the graphics card.
- A recognition model for your own side that cannot hear one of the languages under "I also speak" can now be chosen: that language is not heard for the time being, and its button says why.
- Fixed: on some Windows versions (Windows 10) downloading X-ASR ended with "The engine could not be unpacked".

**Languages**
- Every sentence of yours shows in all the languages of the run, in one order: your language, their main language, their other language lit, whichever of them you said it in. In the conversation and above your avatar in VRChat alike, and for what you type.
- A line too long for the VRChat chatbox goes up in pages; no line of it is dropped.

**VRChat**
- Fixed: "Turn on OSC in VRChat's quick menu" was shown while VRChat was not running.
