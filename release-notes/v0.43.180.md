## 更新内容

**用自己的声音朗读**
- 朗读放在"另一台设备"时也能用自己的音色了：借用那台 Kotomimi 的 Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base，读出来的是你在这台电脑上录的声音。两台都要更新到这一版。
- 你的录音只在对方共享期间临时放在那台电脑上，对方停止共享或退出就清掉；对方自己的音色不会被别的设备用到。
- 添加音色时可以一次录几种语言：录完一种，换语言接着录，保存成同一个音色。
- 录音时照着读的句子换成了几句简短好读的话，不再是一个长句。

**借用另一台 Kotomimi 识别**
- 让那台电脑自动判断语言时，它现在也只在你设定的几种语言里判断；哪种都对不上的一段会显示成灰色，不再被当成别的语言。两台都要更新到这一版。

## What's new

**Read aloud in your own voice**
- With the voice on Another device, it can now be your own: it borrows that Kotomimi's Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base and speaks in the voice you recorded on this computer. Both computers need this version.
- Your recording stays on that computer only while it shares, and is removed when it stops sharing or quits; its own voices are never used by another device.
- Several languages can be recorded in one go when adding a voice: read one, change the language, read the next, and save them as one voice.
- The sentences to read aloud are now a few short easy ones instead of one long sentence.

**Recognition by another Kotomimi**
- Left to detect the language, that computer now does so among the languages you set; a stretch that fits none of them is shown dimmed instead of being taken for another language. Both computers need this version.
