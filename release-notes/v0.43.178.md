## 更新内容

**朗读翻译**
- 修复了朗读译文时断断续续、像电流声的问题。

**回声（按音量）**
- 用电脑自带扬声器时（比如 MacBook），对方的声音和 Kotomimi 自己的朗读不会再被当成你说的话。这种情况下扬声器出声期间你的麦克风会被按住，想同时说话请戴耳机。
- Kotomimi 自己朗读的声音在所有电脑上都会被排除，只开麦克风时也一样。
- 每次开始会话后，头两三句用来测量回声，可能还会漏进来。

## What's new

**Spoken translations**
- Fixed the choppy, crackling sound in spoken translations.

**Echo (by loudness)**
- On a computer's own loudspeakers (a MacBook, say), the other side's voice and Kotomimi's own reading are no longer taken for your speech. Your microphone is held while the loudspeakers sound there; wear headphones to talk at the same time.
- Kotomimi's own reading is kept out on every computer, also with the microphone alone.
- The first two or three sentences of a session measure the echo and may still come through.
