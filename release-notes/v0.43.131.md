## 更新内容

- Mac 的 Apple 语音识别可以同时听两种语言：语言对里两种语言的 Apple 语音识别都下载了，每句话会自动按听出来的语言处理。开着「我也说××语」时说对方的语言会翻回自己的语言；开着语法反馈时说自己的语言会照常翻译；对方说你的语言时原样显示。
- VRChat 头顶聊天框：你用对方的语言说的话直接发原话。模式是「对方」时卡片会提示你说的话不会显示在头顶，并可一键改成「两者」。
- VRChat 放视频时问了没人回答（比如人在 VR 里），12 秒后自动按「加快」处理。「也翻译视频」只在 Kotomimi 下显示。
- 重新运行设置向导时，保存的提供商和各环节的去处不会再被改掉；向导和引导不再提翻译语音。
- 开着「我也说××语」时，反方向需要的翻译模型会出现在翻译卡片里，缺了会提示下载。
- 局域网共享字幕不再把语法反馈当成译文发出去。

## What's new

- The Mac's Apple Speech can hear two languages at once: with both of the pair's languages downloaded, each sentence is handled in the language it was heard in. With "I speak X too", a sentence in the other side's language is translated back into yours; with grammar feedback, one in your own is translated as usual; the other side speaking your language is shown as said.
- VRChat chatbox: what you say in the other side's language goes up as you said it. In the "Them" mode the card now says your words won't show above your avatar, with a button to switch to Both.
- A question about a VRChat video that nobody answers (you in VR) is answered with "Faster" after 12 seconds. "Translate the video too" shows only for Kotomimi.
- Running the setup wizard again no longer changes the saved provider or where each stage runs; the wizard and tour no longer talk of a translated voice.
- With "I speak X too", the translation model needed for the way back now has its row in the translation card, and is asked for when missing.
- Shared LAN captions no longer carry grammar feedback as the translation.
