## 更新内容

- VRChat 头顶聊天框里，原文和译文各占一行，更容易看清。
- 可以选择原文在上还是译文在上。
- VRChat 设置卡片改用聊天框小图来选择显示方式（关、译文、原文在上、译文在上），下方预览聊天框实际的样子。

## What's new

- In VRChat's chatbox, the original and its translation each get a line of their own, which makes them easier to read.
- You can choose whether the original or the translation goes on top.
- The VRChat card now chooses the chatbox from pictures of it (off, translation, original on top, translation on top), with a preview below of how it will look.
