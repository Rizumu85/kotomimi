## 更新内容

- 改进：用另一台设备识别时，如果那台设备没有能识别这个语言的模型，它的共享卡片上会直接出现这个语言和对应模型的“下载”按钮，不用再先改那台设备的语言。
- 改进：另一台设备的语音识别正被别的模型占用时，提示会说它正在忙，不再说那边没有模型、要你去下载。
- 修复：用另一台设备识别、两路同时开始时，偶尔有一路被拒绝，提示“另一台设备上没有能自动分辨语言的识别模型”，其实模型已经下好了。
- 修复：电脑自己用苹果语音识别开会话时，另一台设备就没法换用这台电脑的其他识别模型。
- 修复：个人词典里的词有时会被当成一句话显示出来，例如一串“VRC、糖豆…”。
- 修复：说了识别模型听不懂的语言时，偶尔整句只剩个人词典里的词，例如说俄语被识别成“The VRC”。现在会不带个人词典再识别一遍。

## What's new

- Improved: when another device listens for you and has no model for the language, its sharing card now shows that language with a Download button for the model, so its own language no longer has to be changed first.
- Improved: when the other device's speech recognition is in use with another model, the notice says it is busy instead of asking you to download a model it already has.
- Fixed: with another device listening, starting both sides at once sometimes had one refused as "no recognition model that tells languages apart" although the model was downloaded.
- Fixed: while this computer ran its own session with Apple's speech recognition, another device could not use this computer's other recognition models.
- Fixed: words from the personal dictionary were sometimes shown as a sentence of their own, such as a list like "VRC, …".
- Fixed: speech in a language the recognition model does not follow sometimes came out as nothing but dictionary words, such as Russian read as "The VRC". Such a reading is now made again without the dictionary.
