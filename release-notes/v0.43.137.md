## 更新内容

- 新增（试用）：在 VR 头显里看字幕。打开“设置 → VRChat → 在头显里显示（SteamVR）”后，对方说的话会显示在视线下方。低头看手腕会出现一个小面板，用另一只手的手柄戳一下就能换对方的语言、暂停，还能试用不同的模型。只支持 Windows 和 SteamVR。
- 新增：这台电脑上的模型可以听写和翻译粤语。
- 新增：粤语可以显示粤拼。
- 改进：底栏的音波换成每个按钮上的一个小圆点，有声音时亮起。空闲时更省电。

## What's new

- New (trial): captions in a VR headset. Turn on Settings → VRChat → Show in the headset (SteamVR): what the other side says appears below your line of sight, and a small panel on your wrist, pressed with the other controller, switches their language, pauses, and lets you try other models. Windows and SteamVR only.
- New: this computer's models recognize and translate Cantonese.
- New: Jyutping over Cantonese.
- Improved: the footer's waveforms are now a small dot on each button that lights up when it hears sound, and the app uses less power while idle.
