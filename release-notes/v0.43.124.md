## 更新内容

- **VRChat 联动**（Windows）：设置的「常规」里新增「VRChat」卡片，需要在 VRChat 快捷菜单中打开 OSC。
  - **VRChat 静音时不识别我**：在 VRChat 里静音时，不识别你的语音。
  - **头顶显示我的翻译**：你说的话和打的字翻译后显示在头顶的聊天框里，其他玩家可以看到。开着语法反馈时，显示你说的原句。
  - **播放视频时**：可以选询问、照常、加快或暂停对方。选「加快」时，视频声音没有方向（大多数世界的播放器如此），会自动去掉视频声，只听玩家说话；有方向时，把对方的话切短，译文更快出来。
  - **也翻译视频**：「加快」下面的开关。打开后，视频说的话也会单独翻译，显示得淡一些、小一些。

## What's new

- **VRChat** (Windows): a new "VRChat" card under Settings → General; turn on OSC in VRChat's quick menu.
  - **Don't hear me while muted in VRChat**: muted in VRChat, Kotomimi does not hear you.
  - **Show my translation above my avatar**: what you say or type is translated and shown in the chatbox for other players. With grammar feedback on, it shows the sentence as you said it.
  - **When a video plays**: ask, as usual, faster, or pause the other side. With "Faster", a video whose sound has no direction in the world (most worlds' players) is removed and only the players are heard; otherwise the other side is heard in shorter stretches, so translations come sooner.
  - **Also translate the video**: a switch under "Faster". The video's words are translated too, shown smaller and fainter.
