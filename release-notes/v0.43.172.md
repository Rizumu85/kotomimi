## 更新内容

**词典**
- Qwen3-ASR 不会再把词典里的词写到没人说的地方。以前只要那个位置有差不多长的声音，词典词就会被留下；现在还要那段声音听起来像这个词才保留，否则显示实际听到的内容。
- 只有一个词典词的短句也按同样的标准判断。
- 名字被听错时照旧写成词典里的写法（比如 唐豆 → 糖豆）。

## What's new

**Dictionary**
- Qwen3-ASR no longer writes a word of your dictionary where nobody said it. A word used to stay whenever there was sound of about its length in its place; now that sound also has to resemble the word, or what was actually heard is shown.
- A short sentence of one dictionary word alone is judged the same way.
- A name that was misheard is still written as your dictionary writes it.
