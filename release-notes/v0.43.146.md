## 更新内容

- 新功能：模型可以放到别的盘。设置 →「提供商」最下面的「模型存储」可以更改位置，已下载的模型会一起移过去，不用重新下载；C 盘空间不够时，模型卡片上也会提示并可以直接换位置。
- 改进：用音箱外放时，漏进麦克风的对方声音不会再被当成你说的话。开着「两者」时，Kotomimi 会把你这边和对方同时听到、内容相同的句子去掉，所以回声消除可以用「仅 Kotomimi」，你的声音不会再被一起压低。
- 修复：「对方」选了某个程序（比如 VRChat）时，如果 Kotomimi 启动时它还没出声，会悄悄改成录整个系统的声音，把你自己放的音乐也翻译了。现在会等那个程序出声再录，程序重启后也会自己接上。
- 修复：下载模型前会先检查盘里的空间，不会再下到一半因为空间不足而失败。

## What's new

- New: models can be kept on another drive. Settings → Provider → Model storage changes the location and takes the downloaded models along, so nothing is downloaded again; a model card on a drive without room says so and moves them in place.
- Improved: with loudspeakers, the other side's voice coming back through the microphone is no longer taken for yours. With Both on, a sentence your side and the other side heard at the same moment, reading the same, is dropped, so echo cancellation can stay on "Kotomimi only" and your own voice is no longer turned down with theirs.
- Fixed: with an application chosen for the other side (VRChat, say), Kotomimi captured the whole system when that application was not playing yet as it started, and translated your own music. It now waits for the application to play, and follows it when it restarts.
- Fixed: a model download checks the room on the drive first, rather than failing halfway when it fills.
