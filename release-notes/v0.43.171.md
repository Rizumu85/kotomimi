## 更新内容

**识别**
- 一句话被听成你没点亮的语言时，不再整句消失。它会变暗显示出来，带上识别器认为的语言，让你知道"听到了，但听错了"。
- 这样的句子不会被翻译或纠错，也不会发到 VRChat 聊天框或局域网字幕的观众那里。
- 如果你其实就是在说那种语言，在「我说」里把它点亮就行。

## What's new

**Recognition**
- A sentence heard as a language you have not lit no longer disappears. It is shown dimmed, with the language the recognizer took it for, so you can tell it was heard, and misheard.
- Such a sentence is not translated or given feedback, and is not sent to the VRChat chatbox or to LAN caption viewers.
- If you really were speaking that language, light it under "I speak".
