## 更新内容

**朗读翻译（新）**
- Kotomimi 模式可以把翻译读出来：可以用微软 Edge 在线语音（不占电脑性能），也可以用本机的 Qwen3-TTS 或 Irodori 语音模型。可以选只有你听、对方听（经虚拟声卡送进 VRChat），或者都听。
- 对话里每一句都有一个喇叭按钮，点一下就能听这句怎么读。

**识别**
- 修复：开着「原声直通」时，你说的话会被一段一段压低，很多句子识别不出来。现在不再使用浏览器的回声消除。
- 回声消除改成两档：「关」（戴耳机时用）和「按音量」（用扬声器时用，默认）。「按音量」跟着电脑正在播放的声音，把扬声器漏进麦克风的声音挡在外面，你的声音不受影响。
- 浏览器自带的降噪不再叠在 Kotomimi 的降噪上，降噪选「关」就是真的关。
- Mac 的 Apple Speech：你会说的每种语言各用一个识别器来听（Mac 最多三种，iPad 两种），一句话是什么语言由识别器判断，不再按字形猜。需要的语言没下载时会提示。
- 修复：用 API 识别时，连续说话超过 15 秒被切开的地方会丢掉最后一秒左右的话。

**字幕**
- 你自己那一行的句子按顺序显示，等翻译显示够了再换下一句，翻译几乎不会再来不及看就消失。
- 假名、拼音和罗马音的位置提前留好，第一次说话时字幕窗口不再上下跳动。
- 语法反馈留在它自己那一行；会话记录会保存反馈和每一种翻译。

## What's new

**Spoken translations (new)**
- Kotomimi mode can speak translations aloud: with Microsoft Edge's online voices, which cost the computer nothing, or with the Qwen3-TTS or Irodori voice models on this computer. Choose who hears it: you, the other side (through a virtual cable into VRChat), or both.
- Every line in the conversation has a speaker button that plays how it is said.

**Recognition**
- Fixed: with Original Audio Passthrough on, your speech was turned down in pieces and many sentences were not recognized. The browser's echo cancellation is no longer used.
- Echo cancellation now has two settings: Off, for headphones, and By loudness, for loudspeakers and the default. By loudness follows what the computer is playing and keeps the loudspeakers' sound out of the microphone, leaving your voice as it is.
- The browser's own noise suppression is no longer stacked on Kotomimi's: Noise Suppression set to Off is off.
- Apple Speech on a Mac: each language you speak is heard by a recognizer of its own (up to three on a Mac, two on an iPad), and a sentence's language is the recognizer's, not guessed from its letters. A language that is needed but not downloaded is pointed out.
- Fixed: with an API recognizing, about the last second before a cut in more than 15 seconds of continuous talk was lost.

**Subtitles**
- Your own row shows its sentences in turn and keeps each until its translation has been shown long enough, so translations almost never vanish unread.
- Room for furigana, pinyin and romanization is kept from the start, so the subtitle window no longer jumps at your first sentence.
- Grammar feedback stays on its own line, and the session records keep feedback and every translation.
