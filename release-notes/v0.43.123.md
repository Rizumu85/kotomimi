## 更新内容

- **测试速度**：在设置中"可以开始了"一行点击"测试速度"，即可测出各环节从说完一句到显示原文、译文或语法反馈所需的时间。只在点击时运行；某个环节跟不上说话速度时，会在该环节的卡片中给出建议。支持 50 种语言。
- "逐句翻译"（原"一句一译"）的说明写明了开启和关闭各自的利弊。
- 语法反馈的开关改为"我说对方的语言时检查语法"。

## What's new

- **Speed test**: press "Test speed" beside "Ready to start" in the settings to measure how long each step takes, from the end of a sentence to its text, translation or grammar feedback. It runs only when pressed, and a step that cannot keep up with speech gets a suggestion in its card. 50 languages.
- "Translate sentence by sentence" now explains what turning it on or off costs.
- The grammar feedback switch now reads "Check my grammar when I speak their language".
