## 更新内容

**语言**
- 修复：切换「语音识别」在哪里运行时，「我也会说」里的语言可能被悄悄清空，语法反馈和翻译回去的组合随之失效。现在不会再清空；如果你的已经没了，请在「我说」里重新加一次。
- 开着语法反馈时，你用对方语言说的话也会翻回你的母语，反馈显示在旁边。VRChat 聊天框发的仍是你说的原话。

**提示**
- "识别跟不上，换个更轻的方案？"只在这台电脑自己的模型跟不上时出现。用另一台设备、API 或云服务时不再显示。

## What's new

**Languages**
- Fixed: changing where speech recognition runs could silently empty "I also speak", and feedback together with a translation back stopped working with it. It is kept now; if yours is gone, add the language again under "I speak".
- With grammar feedback on, what you say in the other side's language is also translated back into your own, the feedback beside it. The VRChat chatbox still sends the sentence as you said it.

**Notices**
- "Recognition is falling behind, switch to a lighter plan?" appears only when this computer's own models fall behind, not with another device, an API or a cloud provider.
