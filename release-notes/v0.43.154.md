## 更新内容

- 翻译语言改成两行："我说"和"对方说"。点一下"我说"里的语言就换成你的母语；"对方说"里的语言点一下就点亮或熄灭，最多亮两种。不再自动判断对方换了哪种语言，改成手动切换。
- 对方亮着两种语言时，会按这两种语言听对方，你说的话也会翻成这两种语言，头顶显示两行译文；放不下时先省掉原文。腕表上也能直接点亮或熄灭。
- "我说"里除了母语，只有对方正在用的语言才会用来识别你说话，其余的暗着备用，填多少种语言都不会让识别变乱。对方亮着两种、你又会其中一种时，你用这种语言说的话会翻成另一种。

## What's new

- The translation languages are now two rows, "I speak" and "They speak". Press a language under "I speak" to make it yours; press one under "They speak" to light it or put it out, two at most. Kotomimi no longer guesses which language the other side has switched to: you switch it by hand.
- With two of theirs lit, they are heard in both and what you say is translated into both, as two lines in the chatbox; where they do not fit, the original is left out. The wrist panel lights them too.
- Of the other languages under "I speak", only those the other side is using are listened for; the rest stay dimmed, so a long list no longer muddles recognition. With two of theirs lit and you speaking one of them, what you say in it is translated into the other.
